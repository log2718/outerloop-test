### Takeaways
1. Larger capacity (16, 32 units) causes severe overfitting on this dataset—it's capacity-limited, not underfit
2. Learning rate tuning on small datasets is more effective than architectural changes
3. Depth (adding layers) hurts; single-layer is correct
