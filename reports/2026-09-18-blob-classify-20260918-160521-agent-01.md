### Why It Underperformed
The problem has 20 input features (10 pure noise) with only 400 training samples. With hidden_size=8, the model creates a severe information bottleneck: 20→8→4. The small capacity forces the model to discard most information, making it impossible to separate the signal from noise effectively. Doubling capacity to 32 units helps, but the small dataset limits generalization.

### Takeaways
1. Small training set (400 samples, 4 classes) with high noise-to-signal ratio is fundamentally limiting
2. Larger models need longer training but also require regularization to prevent severe overfitting
3. Marginal improvements are hard to achieve without modifying the optimizer (e.g., weight decay, learning rate schedules) or training loop (e.g., data shuffling, batch processing, early stopping)
